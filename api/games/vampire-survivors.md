## Vampire Survivors Overview

Vampire Survivors tasks players with surviving against endless dark hordes. This is a roguelike action game where thousands of enemies flood the screen. The game's appeal comes from its simple yet addictive mechanics: your character attacks on its own, and you must move strategically to survive as long as possible. The core objective is to become stronger by collecting experience, unlocking new skills, and defeating as many monsters as you can.

### Player Mission

Vampire Survivors players start with a basic character and simple abilities. Your mission is to move skillfully, avoiding enemy attacks while eliminating them to collect experience gems. As you collect enough points, your character levels up, allowing you to select new skills like weapons, buffs, or special powers. A key strategy is to choose skill combinations that maximize your power, since the number and strength of enemies steadily increase. Every run offers a unique experience, demanding constant adaptation to survive longer.

### Controls

Vampire Survivors controls are straightforward: Use W, A, S, D to move your character. The mouse is used to navigate menus and select upgrade skills.
