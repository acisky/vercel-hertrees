## Soyjak Siege: Bullet Hell Defense Game

Soyjak Siege is a top-down bullet hell game released in 2026 by OzGames. Players control Chud, defending the last Gem against relentless waves of Soyjak enemies attacking from all directions. This is a survival-focused experience where dodging, leveling up, and strategic character building determine how long you can last against the meme horde.

## Game Overview

Soyjak Siege throws players into an arena with continuous enemy spawning. The character automatically shoots at nearby targets, allowing players to concentrate entirely on movement and evasion. Enemy kills drop EXP orbs that grant level-ups and unlock upgrade choices, creating a progression system that rewards survival.

## Core Gameplay Mechanics

Players navigate the arena using mouse movement to avoid incoming projectiles and enemy contact. The hero automatically engages nearby threats without manual input. Collecting EXP orbs triggers level-ups, presenting upgrade options that modify abilities and stats. Survival depends on enduring increasingly difficult waves and defeating challenging boss encounters. Extended survival results in progressively denser bullet patterns and enemy counts filling the screen.
