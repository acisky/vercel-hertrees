## Lab Havoc – Sandbox Ragdoll Chaos

Lab Havoc is a physics-based sandbox ragdoll game developed by OFree Games. Players start in a fully equipped lab filled with human clones, weapons, traps, and gadgets. The core objective is simple: build experiments and watch the ragdoll chaos play out. Ragdoll physics ensure every single run delivers unpredictable, laugh-out-loud results.

## Why Lab Havoc Is Fun

Experimentation is at the heart of Lab Havoc. Explosives, spikes, and firearms all interact in unexpected ways — clones might fly across the room, tumble into a forgotten trap, or collide with multiple hazards in one run. Cartoon-style physics keep the tone light, but the real appeal is watching each custom setup produce a completely different outcome. No two experiments ever play out the same way.

### Experimenting With Weapons and Traps

Start playing by selecting traps and weapons from the available arsenal, then place them anywhere inside the test chamber. Mines, spikes, guns, and more can be positioned and repositioned freely before releasing a clone. Timing plays a key role here — releasing the clone too early or too late could produce a totally different chain reaction than expected.

Once activated, ragdoll physics take full control. Watch the clone bounce, fall, or get launched across the chamber by your trap layout. Try to tweak and refine your setup after each run — this is part puzzle, part pure chaos.

### Unlock New Gadgets

Complete experiments and reach damage milestones to unlock new weapons and tools. Each new gadget opens up more creative possibilities within the sandbox. More unlocked equipment means more chances to build ridiculous ragdoll setups and push the chaos even further.

### Controls

- **Drag & Drop** – Place weapons and traps anywhere in the chamber
- **Mouse / Tap** – Release clones into the experiment
- Rearrange all elements freely before activating for maximum effect

### Tips for Maximum Mayhem

- Mix different trap types together — combinations produce the most unpredictable ragdoll results
- Try to experiment with release timing; small delays could lead to much bigger reactions
- Must rearrange setups between runs to discover what the physics engine can really do
- Unlock new weapons early — this gives more options and makes each experiment crazier

## Keep Playing Lab Havoc

Lab Havoc is chaotic, funny, and built for endless replayability. Each experiment produces a different outcome, and every new gadget unlocks fresh ways to build and destroy. This is a sandbox that rewards creativity and curiosity. Whether playing in short sessions or long stretches, every run in Lab Havoc feels new — and that's what keeps players coming back.
