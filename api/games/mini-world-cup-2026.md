## Mini World Cup 2026: Predict & Win

Mini World Cup 2026 is a sports prediction game where players compete to correctly forecast match results, scorers, and tournament outcomes from the 2026 World Cup held across the United States, Mexico, and Canada.

### What Is Mini World Cup 2026?

Mini World Cup 2026 is a prediction game that runs alongside the actual 2026 World Cup tournament. Instead of passively watching matches across the US, Mexico, and Canada, players get to compete against other fans by forecasting match outcomes.

The concept is simple: predict which teams will win, lose, or draw. Players could also guess exact scores and call which players will find the net. Points accumulate based on prediction accuracy. A correct basic result earns three points. An exact scoreline is worth seven points. Each correctly named goal scorer adds two points. This is also a 15-point bonus opportunity for anyone who predicts everything correctly on a single matchday.

The scoring system rewards both safe picks and risky gambles, making every decision count. Players could play it conservative and build steady points, or go bold on long shots to climb the leaderboard faster.

### How to Play

#### Getting Started

Mini World Cup 2026 takes just a couple of minutes to set up. Start playing on the Miniplay platform by creating a free account, then find the Mini World Cup 2026 game page to begin. Before the tournament starts, players must lock in their Global Picks — predictions for the overall winner, top three finishers, and tournament top scorer. These must be submitted before July 1st, so try to complete them early.

#### Match Predictions

For each match, players can submit three types of predictions:

- 1X2 picks (which team wins or if it's a draw)
- Exact score
- Goal scorer predictions (up to 5 players per match)

Submit predictions before kickoff, watch the action unfold, and see points update once the final whistle blows. The leaderboard refreshes after every game, so players always know where they stand.

#### Strategy Tips

Mini World Cup 2026 turns every match into something worth paying attention to. The real skill is knowing when to play it safe and when to take a shot. Some matches are clear mismatches where bold predictions make sense. Others are total toss-ups where a straightforward 1X2 pick could be the best move. Try to read each fixture carefully before committing to a prediction.
